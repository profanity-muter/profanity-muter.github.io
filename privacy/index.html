Profanity Muter

Privacy policy

Profanity Muter, published by Urban Algorithm LLC. Last updated 2026-11-14.

The extension

Profanity Muter listens to the audio of YouTube videos on your device and mutes swearing in real time. The speech model is bundled inside the extension. Everything it hears and everything you watch stays on your device. The extension does not send audio, transcripts, video titles, links, or your viewing history anywhere, and it has no account, analytics, or advertising.

The extension stores your settings (strictness, word lists, optional password lock) and simple on-device counts for your own activity screen in your browser's extension storage. If you use Chrome sync, Chrome may sync the settings through your Google account. Profanity Muter does not receive that data.

Feedback pages

If you uninstall Profanity Muter, or tap the feedback link in its popup, your browser opens our feedback page at profanity-muter.github.io/feedback. The page address includes only the version number and where you came from (uninstall or paused). Answering is optional.

  • Reason taps are counted anonymously with Umami, a self-hosted analytics tool. The page sets no cookies, and no IP addresses are stored.
  • If you choose to send a comment, it goes to our support inbox through Formspree. We use it only to improve Profanity Muter and delete it after 12 months. You can also add a video link and, if you would like a reply, your email address. Both are optional. We use the email address only to answer you, and it is deleted with the comment.
  • The page is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may keep ordinary server logs under its own policy.
