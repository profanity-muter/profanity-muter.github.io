Common causes of missed words

  • Shorts, livestreams and premieres are not filtered.
  • It works on youtube.com only, not on YouTube Music or on videos embedded in other sites.
  • A YouTube tab that was already open when you installed needs one refresh.
  • Only words on your active list are muted. You can check the list and add your own in the extension's popup.
  • Analysis trails the video, so the first seconds and the moments right after you skip may not be checked yet.
  • Speech recognition can mishear a word, most often over music.
